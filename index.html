About Me

My research lies at the intersection of computer graphics and computer vision, with a focus on learning 3D representations from images and videos. I develop methods for reconstructing, rendering, and editing 3D scenes and digital humans, with particular interests in neural rendering, inverse rendering, and relightable, animatable avatars. My goal is to make realistic 3D content creation more efficient, accessible, and controllable.

My first name in Chinese is pronounced very similar to Kevin in English, which is, therefore, my English name. In spare time, I am a huge fan of Cyber Literature (over ten years of experience), and I occasionally play Screeps for fun (you can find my AI here!).
